Company VPN Guide

Employees must connect to the company VPN when accessing internal
company resources from outside the office network.

To connect to the VPN:

1. Open the company VPN application.
2. Enter your company username.
3. Enter your company password.
4. Select the Corporate VPN profile.
5. Click Connect.
6. Complete MFA verification when prompted.

If the VPN connection fails, restart the VPN application and check
that your internet connection is working.

If the problem continues, contact the IT Helpdesk.
